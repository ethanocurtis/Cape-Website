        How do I get a webhook URL?
        1. In Discord, open Server Settings → Integrations → Webhooks. You need the Manage Webhooks permission.
        2. Click New Webhook and pick the channel for alerts.
        3. Click Copy Webhook URL and paste it above.

        Keep the URL private: anyone who has it can post in that channel.
